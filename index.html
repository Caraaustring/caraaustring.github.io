Selected work

Innovation Foundry

A 3D printing education business I founded in 2022 and still run.

I built the courses and teach most of the classes myself. Classes are capped at 12 students, and summer camps (capped at 15, mornings and afternoons) have sold out all 9 weeks. About 275 students ages 8 to 12 have come through in four years, and many return every year.

I interview, train, and schedule the team: one full-time employee and 12 seasonal staff in the summer. I now work on it remotely from USC while my brother runs the main role.

Brain-controlled AR hand

A brain-computer interface that predicts which hand you’re about to raise.

In August 2025 I connected a Neurosity Crown to an AR hand animation I made in Spline. A Python program learns from a short training session, then predicts which hand the user is raising.

W17 slot-car track

A car modeled from scratch, 3D printed, and driven around a track I built.

From March to May 2026, I modeled the W17 in OnShape and printed it. Then I put a motor inside the car and powered the track, so it runs like a slot car.

Downtown Summit scavenger hunt

An ESP32 clue hunt that sends people to local businesses.

To bring foot traffic to businesses in downtown Summit, NJ, I modeled a suitcase-style case for the electronics and programmed an ESP32 to reveal the next clue when it comes within range of the ESP32 at the next business.

About

I started Innovation Foundry in 2022 to teach kids 3D printing, and I haven’t stopped making things since: CAD models, microcontroller projects, AR animations, and sets for my school’s plays and musicals.

I like the space between a working prototype and something people enjoy using. I’m interested in automotive and product design, AR and VR, and STEM education for young people.
